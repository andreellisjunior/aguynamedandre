## Overview

Earned It helps families replace repeated reminders and scattered chore charts with one clear, motivating system. Parents assign chores and coin values, kids submit completed work, and rewards make progress tangible without taking control away from the adults.

The experience treats household responsibilities like a quest: kids always know what to do next, what their effort is worth, and how close they are to a reward they care about.

## Key Features

### Chores That Run Themselves

- Create one-time or recurring chores with schedules and coin values
- Add multi-step checklists for jobs that need a clear definition of done
- Send reminders when chores are due, submitted, or awaiting approval

### Photo Proof and Parent Approval

- Kids can attach a photo and note when submitting a chore
- Parents approve completed work or request a redo with feedback
- Coins are awarded only after approval, keeping parents in control

### A Reward Store Kids Care About

- Parents create custom rewards such as screen time, outings, or a saved-for purchase
- Visual progress bars show kids how close they are to each goal
- Reward redemptions require approval before coins leave a child's balance

### One View for the Whole Family

- A parent dashboard summarizes children, recent activity, streaks, and weekly progress
- Co-parent accounts share the same approvals, ledger, and reward data
- A reversible transaction history keeps every coin award and redemption accountable

### Motivation That Builds Consistency

- Streaks and badges reward follow-through over time
- Clear progress replaces nagging with self-directed motivation
- The responsive experience works across the phones a family already uses

## Product Principles

- Make the next responsibility obvious
- Reward effort only after a parent verifies the result
- Let anticipation and visible progress do the motivating
- Keep every family member aligned around a single source of truth
