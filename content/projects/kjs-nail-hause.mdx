## Overview

Hello and welcome! I’m thrilled you’ve chosen me as your nail technician. With over 4 years of professional experience in the nail industry, I am passionate about creating stunning, personalized nail designs. My career includes serving as a nail instructor at various beauty schools across northern Illinois and Indiana, where I’ve had the opportunity to mentor the next generation of nail technicians. From bold, long “baddie” nails to timeless French tips or sleek, no-chip manicures, I specialize in crafting the perfect look tailored to each client’s style. I look forward to working with you to achieve your ideal nails.
