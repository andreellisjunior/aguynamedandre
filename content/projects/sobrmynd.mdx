## Overview

SobrMynd is a deliberately restrained devotional experience built around a simple rhythm: slow down, open the Bible, consider one thoughtful question, and continue the reflection away from the screen.

Rather than trying to replace Scripture or turn spiritual practice into an engagement loop, the app creates a gentle starting point and then gets out of the way.

## Key Features

### Scripture First

- Presents a verse for the day as the beginning of reflection
- Encourages readers to open their own Bible and explore the surrounding passage
- Keeps the interface calm and focused on the text rather than app mechanics

### One Thoughtful Question

- Offers a single prompt to carry into prayer
- Gives users a focused theme for personal reflection
- Supports writing in a physical journal instead of keeping attention inside the app

### Intentionally Low Pressure

- No streaks or gamified pressure
- No crowded feed or endless devotional content
- A short, purposeful journey designed to end naturally

### Private, Simple Access

- A focused sign-in flow provides access to the experience
- Minimal navigation keeps the path from Scripture to reflection clear
- Responsive design makes the daily journey comfortable on mobile devices

## Product Principles

- Point toward Scripture rather than summarize it away
- Ask one good question instead of supplying every answer
- Respect the user's attention and spiritual practice
- Measure success by meaningful time away from the app
